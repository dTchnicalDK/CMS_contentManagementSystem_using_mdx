# Indus Valley Civilization

The Indus Valley Civilization was one of the earliest urban civilizations in the world, known for its advanced city planning. Its major cities, including Harappa and Mohenjo-daro, were laid out on a precise grid, with wide streets running north-south and east-west.

<AssetRef
  id="indusvalley-sitemap-279e"
  caption="Map of major Indus Valley Civilization sites."
/>

One of the civilization's most striking achievements was its drainage system. Nearly every house had access to covered drains that carried wastewater away from the city, a level of urban sanitation not matched in the region for centuries afterward.
